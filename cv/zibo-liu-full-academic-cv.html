Research profile

Zibo Liu is an acoustics researcher working across physical acoustics, acoustic metamaterials, computational design, and human-centred soundscape engineering. He combines wave-physics modelling, numerical and data-driven methods, experiments, and perceptual evaluation. He is Principal Investigator of the Swedish Research Council-funded SMILE project at KTH.
