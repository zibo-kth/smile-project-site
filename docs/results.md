# Publications and resources

<p class="page-lead">This page separates public SMILE outputs from related research that helped establish the project’s scientific foundation.</p>

## SMILE project outputs

SMILE began on **1 January 2026**. Peer-reviewed publications, software, datasets, demonstrators, and guidance will be listed here after scientific quality assurance and, where relevant, ethics, licensing, and collaboration review.

<div class="callout">
  <strong>Release principle.</strong> Public outputs will identify what was tested, the supported operating or design range, relevant uncertainty, and any restrictions on reuse. No public release is listed before it is ready.
</div>
