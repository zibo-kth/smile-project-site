# SMILE

**SMILE: Ljudlandskapsutformning med Metamaterialinnovation och Lärande**<br>
<strong class="project-name-cjk">SMILE-乐景工程</strong>

**Soundscape engineering with Metamaterial Innovation and LEarning** is a Swedish Research Council research project led at KTH Royal Institute of Technology (2026–2029).

![Abstract illustration connecting people, sound, and engineered structures](assets/smile-hero-art.svg)

SMILE explores how human-centred soundscape research and acoustic metamaterials can contribute to better acoustic environments. The project brings together three broad themes:

- soundscape perception and evaluation;
- physical acoustics and acoustic metamaterials;
- computational support for engineering design.

Zibo Liu drives and leads the Swedish Research Council-funded project at KTH. The work develops with support from collaborators and researchers across acoustics, soundscape, and computational design.

This website is the project’s public dissemination space. It presents the overall vision, people, news, and released results. Detailed methods, designs, and unpublished findings will be shared through scientific publications and public outputs when they are ready.

## One project, three perspectives

<div class="feature-grid" markdown>

<article markdown>
### Listen to people

Soundscape research asks how acoustic environments are perceived in context—not only how loud they are.
</article>

<article markdown>
### Shape sound

Acoustic metamaterials use carefully designed structures to influence how sound is transmitted, reflected, or absorbed.
</article>

<article markdown>
### Connect the evidence

Modelling and learning-assisted tools support design exploration, while physical and listening-based evaluation keep the work grounded.
</article>
