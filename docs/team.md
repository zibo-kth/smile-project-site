## Collaboration environment

- **KTH Royal Institute of Technology** administers the grant and hosts the project.
- **University College London** contributes through research collaboration in soundscape and environmental acoustics.
- **Scania** provides an industrial research environment relevant to vehicle acoustics and engineering translation.

Specific author lists, responsibilities, and institutional contributions are reported with each research output.
