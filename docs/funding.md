# Funding

SMILE is funded by the **Swedish Research Council (Vetenskapsrådet)** through the 2025 open call in Natural and Engineering Sciences.

- **Official Swedish title:** *SMILE: Ljudlandskapsutformning med Metamaterialinnovation och Lärande*
- **Grant:** 2025-06253
- **Project period:** 1 January 2026–31 December 2029
- **Project leader:** Zibo Liu
- **Administering organisation:** KTH Royal Institute of Technology

Research links with University College London contribute complementary academic expertise.

[Swedish Research Council](https://www.vr.se/) · [KTH Royal Institute of Technology](https://www.kth.se/en) · [University College London](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/)

KTH announced the award on 3 November 2025. [Read the KTH news item](https://www.kth.se/om/nyheter/centrala-nyheter/kth-forskare-far-155-miljoner-av-vetenskapsradet-1.1437168).
