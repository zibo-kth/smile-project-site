- **Official title:** *SMILE: Ljudlandskapsutformning med Metamaterialinnovation och Lärande*
- **Project leader:** Zibo Liu
- **Administering organisation:** KTH Royal Institute of Technology, Department of Engineering Mechanics
- **Subject area:** Natural Sciences and Engineering
- **Funded period:** 1 January 2026–31 December 2029

## Public record

KTH announced the project among its Swedish Research Council awards on **3 November 2025**. [Read the KTH announcement](https://www.kth.se/om/nyheter/centrala-nyheter/kth-forskare-far-155-miljoner-av-vetenskapsradet-1.1437168).

The award information above is based on the project decision and grant documentation. Research collaborators are described by function so that collaboration is not confused with funding or contractual status.
