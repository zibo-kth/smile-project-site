# Work Packages

The public project structure is organised around three connected areas.

## 1. Soundscape understanding

Study how acoustic environments are experienced and identify questions that are meaningful for engineering research. This connects context, perception, and listening-based evidence with the language used in acoustic design.

## 2. Acoustic concepts

Explore acoustic metamaterials and computational approaches for shaping sound. Modelling and learning-assisted methods can help compare possibilities before selected concepts move to deeper investigation.

## 3. Integration and evaluation

Bring perception, modelling, and experimental research together, then communicate validated outcomes through publications and demonstrators. A central principle is that an improved numerical indicator is not automatically an improved human experience.

## How the areas connect

The three areas form an evidence loop rather than a one-way pipeline: perceptual questions inform acoustic concepts; modelling and experiments reveal what is physically achievable; and evaluation tests whether a change is meaningful in context. Insights can move in either direction as the research develops.

This is intentionally a high-level dissemination summary. Detailed tasks, methods, milestones, candidate designs, and decision criteria are not published here before the corresponding research has been reviewed and released.
