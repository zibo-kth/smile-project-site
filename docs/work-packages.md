# Work Packages

The public project structure is organised around three connected areas.

## 1. Soundscape understanding

Study how acoustic environments are experienced and identify questions that are meaningful for engineering research.

## 2. Acoustic concepts

Explore acoustic metamaterials and computational approaches for shaping sound.

## 3. Integration and evaluation

Bring perception, modelling, and experimental research together, then communicate validated outcomes through publications and demonstrators.

This is intentionally a high-level dissemination summary. Detailed tasks, methods, milestones, candidate designs, and decision criteria are not published here before the corresponding research has been reviewed and released.
